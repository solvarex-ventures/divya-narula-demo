About Divya

Dedication. Expertise. Passion.

A graduate of Delhi University with a post-graduate diploma from Symbiosis College, Pune, Divya has spent over fifteen years in education counselling — working with more than 5,000 students on their path from application to acceptance.

She holds multiple career-counselling certifications and has trained with top-ranked universities worldwide, staying current through education fairs, workshops and forums — with a particular specialisation in art and design guidance.
